## PMP-1. Project and Team

### Project and Jira Setup

**Project in one sentence:** ChildConnect is a web-based decision support platform where parents and specialists share one weekly schedule, task list and record of observations about a child, with a role-based AI summary that supports, but never makes, their decisions.
